I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Computational Cosmology within the Department of Physics at Durham University, where I work with [Professor Sownak Bose](https://sownakbose.github.io/){:target="_blank"}. 

I completed my Ph.D. in Physics at Stanford University, advised by Professor Risa Wechsler as part of the [Galaxy Formation and Cosmology Group](https://gfc.stanford.edu/){:target="_blank"} within the Kavli Insitute for Particle Astrophysics and Cosmology. My research interests broadly include exploring the galaxy-dark matter connection, particularly through structure-formation modeling and galaxy bias. You can view my work on [Google scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&authuser=2&user=cL4njVMAAAAJ){:target="_blank"} or [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/astro-ph?searchtype=author&query=Shiferaw,+M){:target="_blank"}.

I am passionate about outreach and diversity in astronomy and physics. I have organized a [Black in Physics](https://physics.stanford.edu/inclusion/black-physics-stanford){:target="_blank"} group at Stanford University, and have actively engaged in science communication through the [Wonderfest Science Envoys Program](https://wonderfest.org/science-envoy/){:target="_blank"}. I was also a Stanford Data Science Scholar.

In my free time, I love running and making [art](https://www.instagram.com/artbymahlet/){:target="_blank"}.
